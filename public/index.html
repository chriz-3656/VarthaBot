The Purpose

Bridging the gap between journalism and Discord.

Traditional news delivery on Discord is chaotic. Webhooks often spam duplicate articles, lack proper formatting, and overwhelm server members.

VarthaBot was purpose-built to solve this. We act as a highly intelligent middleware that intercepts raw RSS feeds, cleans the data, formats it into a premium editorial layout, and delivers it to your community seamlessly.
